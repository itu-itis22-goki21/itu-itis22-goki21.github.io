Momentune for iPhone

Find the music of the moment.

Take a photo. Momentune looks at the light, colors and mood of the scene, adds the time, season and weather, and builds a music journey that fits it.

A soundtrack for every photo

Momentune turns what you see into what you hear.

From photo to playlist

Take a new photo or pick one from your library. Momentune reads the scene and suggests songs that fit it.

Songs that flow together

Songs are ordered by tempo, so the journey flows smoothly. Momentune can also start a song at its estimated best part.

Apple Music & Spotify

Every song is checked against the catalog. Play it right away, or save the journey as a private playlist in your account.

Fits your context

Time of day, season, weather and your preferred music language all shape the songs you get.

Memories

Save a photo together with its soundtrack. Favorite the songs you love, and Momentune learns your taste over time.

Private by design

Saved photos stay on your device. For analysis, the photo is resized and its location data is removed. It is never stored in the cloud.

How it works

Three steps, a few seconds.

  1. Capture

    Take a photo of the moment you are in, or choose one from your library.

  2. Listen

    Momentune reads the scene and builds a journey of songs that flow together.

  3. Keep

    Play it, save it as a playlist, add it to your memories, or share it to your story.

Your photos are yours

Before the first analysis, Momentune shows you which AI service will process your photo and asks for your permission. Only a resized copy is sent, with location data removed, and only your city and country are added. Saved photos never leave your device. You can delete your account and all synced data from Settings at any time.
