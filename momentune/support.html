Getting started

Which devices does Momentune support?

Momentune runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Why does Momentune ask permission before the first analysis?

To recommend music, a resized copy of your photo, with its location data removed, is sent to an AI service. Before the first analysis, the app shows you which provider is used and asks for your OK. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Do I need location access?

No. Location helps Momentune add local weather and your city to the recommendations, but everything works without it. Only your city and country are sent, never exact coordinates.

Music

Songs don’t play inside the app.

Playing songs inside the app needs a connected Apple Music account with an active Apple Music subscription. Spotify songs open in the Spotify app. Check the connection in Momentune’s Settings › Connections, and make sure Media & Apple Music access is allowed in iPhone Settings › Momentune.

How do I connect or disconnect Spotify or Apple Music?

Open Settings › Connections in Momentune and tap Connect or Disconnect next to the service. You can also choose your default service there.

Some songs were skipped when I saved a playlist.

Every song is checked against the music service’s catalog. If a song isn’t available in your service or region, it is left out, and the app shows how many songs were skipped.

Why does a song start in the middle?

With automatic playback turned on, Momentune can start a song at its estimated best part. If the estimate isn’t confident enough, the song plays from the beginning. You can turn this off in Settings.

Memories and devices

Why don’t my photos appear on my other device?

To protect your privacy, photos stay only on the device where you saved them. Your songs, analysis details and favorites sync between devices. To sync, link your account with Sign in with Apple in Settings.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Link your account with Sign in with Apple in Settings, then sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone. Song and analysis details will sync. Photos are not uploaded, so they won’t be moved.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Momentune and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

I bought Pro but the features are locked.

Open Settings in Momentune and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with. If it still doesn’t work, email us with the user ID shown in Settings.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy and your account

How do I delete my account and data?

In Momentune, go to Settings › Delete account. This permanently removes your synced data, your account and the photos stored on that device. It does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel that separately in iPhone Settings.

Are my photos stored on your servers?

No. Photos sent for analysis are processed in memory and not stored by our servers. Photos you save as memories stay on your device.
